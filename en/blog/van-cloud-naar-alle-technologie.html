The shift from cloud to all technology

Jelle van Zantwijk·14 Aug 2026·4 min read

No IT department saves on 'the cloud' alone any more

Anyone who today still sees FinOps as a trick to lower AWS or Azure bills is behind the facts. The latest figures don't lie: the FinOps Foundation has officially changed its mission from "managing the value of Cloud" to "managing the value of Technology".

That is not wordplay. It is an acknowledgement of what is already happening in practice: IT costs have become completely fragmented across cloud, AI, SaaS, software and own data centres, and that fragmentation calls for one coherent picture instead of separate pots.

IT costs are fragmented

Within organisations, the FinOps team now manages a much broader range than a few years ago:

  • 98% AI spend (was 31% two years ago)
  • 90% SaaS licences
  • 64% software licences
  • 57% private cloud & 48% data centres

The jump in AI spend stands out most: from a niche application to something almost every FinOps team now manages as standard. Anyone who still sees AI costs separately from the rest of the tech stack is by now missing most of the story.

Where the biggest gains are now

At AX1S we now see the biggest gains and cost-effectiveness in the total IT stack, no longer just in cloud. An organisation that only looks at the cloud bill while SaaS licences and AI spend are left untouched structurally leaves savings potential on the table. The free tools and templates from AX1S, including the FinOps Radar and the AI Cost Calculator, have therefore been deliberately built to look at cloud and AI costs together.

Two years ago, AI spend fell under the management of 31% of FinOps teams. Today that is 98%. No other category has gone mainstream within FinOps as fast.
