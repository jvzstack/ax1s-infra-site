Explaining cloud and AI costs in the boardroom

Jelle van Zantwijk·7 Sep 2026·3 min read
Short answer

If cloud and AI costs have risen again and you have to explain it in the boardroom, you are empty-handed without insight. The alternative is to know in advance where every euro of tech spend goes and why. The FinOps Quick-Win Checklist gives 16 concrete actions to make costs visible and reduce them.

Why are cloud and AI costs so hard to explain?

Most CFOs can tell you to the euro what personnel costs are doing. With cloud and AI, that knowledge often runs out. Until the question is asked: why have the costs gone up again?

What is the alternative?

Already knowing where every euro of tech spend goes and why, before you walk into the boardroom. That requires an answer to three questions:

  • What does every euro of tech spend go to: per service, team or product?
  • Why did the amount rise: more volume, a new project or a price change?
  • Which actions are under way to control the costs?

Where do you start?

A good starting point is the FinOps Quick-Win Checklist: 16 concrete actions, from immediate quick wins to structural embedding. Download the checklist (pdf, Dutch version). More templates and tools are available under free tools and templates.

Frequently asked questions

What is the FinOps Quick-Win Checklist?

A free checklist with 16 concrete actions, from immediate quick wins to structurally embedding FinOps. You can print it out and tick them off.

How do you explain increased cloud costs to the board?

By knowing in advance what the costs go to and why they rose: more volume, a new project or a price change, and which actions are under way to control the costs.
