3 signs your AI costs are getting out of hand

Jelle van Zantwijk·18 Aug 2026·3 min read

AI costs scale with users, not with infrastructure

AI costs behave differently from a regular cloud bill. They don't scale with your infrastructure, but with your users. And users simply grow faster than budgets are adjusted.

These are three signs we encounter most often at organisations where the AI bill has already got out of hand, or is about to in the short term.

1. Nobody can answer the question "what does our AI feature cost per user?"

If that question gets a shrug, that is itself the signal. A team building a chatbot or recommendation engine looks at functionality. Nobody calculates by default what each extra user adds in inference costs. At AX1S we see this in almost every intake conversation: the technology works, adoption grows, but the cost per user has never been calculated. With the AI Cost Calculator you can work it out in a few minutes, including the growth projection.

2. The monthly invoice structurally deviates from the previous one, for no clear reason

With regular cloud costs, an increase can usually be traced to a concrete cause: new capacity, a new project. With AI costs that clear trigger is often missing. The invoice simply rises with usage, and nobody has actively tracked that usage. Anyone who has seen this pattern for a few months without an explanation is almost certainly behind the facts.

3. Success first feels like a compliment, and only later like a bill

An AI feature takes off. Users find it valuable, adoption grows, everyone is happy. That very success is what lets the costs quietly grow along, without anyone consciously looking at it. By the time the invoice stands out, the growth is long under way. Not because something went wrong, but because nobody associates success with a rising cost item.

None of these three signs is a problem on its own. The problem arises when nobody recognises them before the bill comes in at three times what was expected.

Do you calculate your AI costs per user now, or only as a total at the bottom of the invoice?
